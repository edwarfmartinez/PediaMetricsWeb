What it calculates

One assessment, multiple pediatric growth indicators

The most useful indicator depends on the child's age, available measurements, and the question being considered. PediaMetrics applies the relevant WHO reference and displays the results that can be calculated from the information entered.

Weight-for-age

Places weight in relation to age using the applicable WHO reference.

Height or length-for-age

Places recumbent length or standing height in relation to age.

Weight-for-length or height

Relates weight to body length or height rather than to age alone.

BMI-for-age

Interprets calculated body mass index relative to age and sex.

Head-circumference-for-age

Provides an age-eligible head-circumference result where supported.

BMI, BSA, and fluids

Keeps body metrics and maintenance-fluid estimates beside the growth assessment.

A safer input workflow

Catch a questionable value before it becomes a confident-looking result.

A misplaced decimal, the wrong unit, or an implausible measurement can make any calculated output misleading. PediaMetrics surfaces relevant checks and withholds unavailable results until the measurements are corrected.

These checks support review; they do not establish a diagnosis or replace repeat measurement and clinical judgment.

Calculator FAQ

Common questions before downloading

Scope, standards, measurement history, and privacy.

What does the calculator calculate?

It calculates applicable WHO Z-scores, percentiles, and classifications for weight-for-age, height or length-for-age, weight-for-length or height, BMI-for-age, and head-circumference-for-age.

Does it check measurements first?

The app includes age-based checks that can flag unlikely values or possible unit-entry mistakes for review.

Can I chart more than one measurement?

Yes. Save patient profiles and measurement histories locally, then review eligible measurements on interactive growth charts.

Does it replace clinical judgment?

No. PediaMetrics is a support tool. Results must be interpreted in context and do not replace professional medical judgment, diagnosis, or treatment decisions.

Where is patient information stored?

Patient profiles and measurement history are stored locally on the user's device. No account is required.
