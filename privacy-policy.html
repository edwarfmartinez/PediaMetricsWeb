Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

PediaMetrics ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the PediaMetrics mobile app and this website.

1. Information We Collect

PediaMetrics is designed to work without requiring user accounts. We do not collect, store, or transmit personal information to our own servers through the app.

If you contact us directly by email, we receive the information you include in your message (such as your email address and message content) so we can respond.

2. Health and Measurement Data

Pediatric measurements entered in the app are processed on-device. We do not sell health data and do not use it for advertising.

3. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

PediaMetrics uses limited analytics to understand how the app is used and to improve its functionality, reliability, and performance. This analytics data relates only to app usage and does not include personal information or any health or measurement data entered in the app.

This website uses Google Analytics 4 to measure page visits, engagement with product information, and clicks that lead to the Apple App Store. Website analytics may process technical and acquisition information such as the page visited, browser or device category, approximate country, referring website, campaign parameters, and the location and wording of an App Store link. Google Analytics may use first-party identifiers for this measurement. Advertising-personalization signals and Google Signals are disabled in the website configuration.

Website analytics does not receive pediatric measurements, patient profiles, or other information entered in the PediaMetrics app. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy.

  • No in-app ads are displayed.
  • No sale of user data.
  • No unnecessary cross-app tracking.
  • Website analytics is used to evaluate product information and App Store conversion, not personalized ads.

4. Third-Party Services and Links

The website uses Google Analytics and may link to third-party services such as the Apple App Store. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Data Retention

We do not collect or retain app-submitted personal information or health records on our servers. Limited, non-personal app-usage analytics may be retained only as needed to analyze and improve the app. Email communications are retained only as needed to provide support and comply with legal obligations. Website analytics data is retained according to the settings and safeguards configured for the Google Analytics property.

6. Children's Privacy

PediaMetrics is a professional health tool intended for adults, including healthcare professionals and parents/caregivers. It is not directed to children as independent users.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: support@valioralabs.com
